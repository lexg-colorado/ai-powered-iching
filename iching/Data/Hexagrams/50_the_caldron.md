# Hexagram 50 — ䷱ 鼎 (dǐng) — The Caldron

**Alternate Names:** "The Caldron", "The Cauldron", "Holding", "The Sacrificial Vessel"

**Upper Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)
**Lower Trigram:** ☴ 巽 (xùn) — Wind (風)

**King Wen Number:** 50 | **Binary:** 101110

---

## Overview

The Caldron symbolizes a vessel of transformation and nourishment. As an instrument for cooking and ceremonial offering, it represents the capacity to receive, contain, and refine. This hexagram speaks to proper function and the refinement of the raw into the sacred.

## Judgment

The Caldron finds success through proper management and respect for its function. What is placed within is transformed through heat and time into nourishment. The superior person maintains the vessel with care, honoring both its practical and spiritual purpose.

## Image

Fire above Wind below suggests rising transformation and proper circulation. The superior person thus stands in her place and fulfills her function with clarity and integrity.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The empty caldron newly appointed awaits its purpose. This is the time of preparation and readiness, when all is clean and ready to receive what is needed.

### Nine in the Second Place

The vessel fills gradually with good substance. One must select carefully what enters, ensuring quality and fitness for the transformative work ahead.

### Nine in the Third Place

The handles break under strain from misuse. When a vessel is employed beyond its proper function or carelessly managed, loss and dysfunction follow inevitably.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The feet are broken and the contents spilled. Carelessness and disrespect for the vessel's function lead to ruin and waste of what was carefully gathered.

### Six in the Fifth Place

The golden handles and jade ornaments adorn the caldron. With proper care and respect, the vessel becomes beautiful and precious, serving both noble function and aesthetic grace.

### Nine at the Top

The caldron carries precious jade handles and is complete in all aspects. When one has perfected the vessel and honored its purpose fully, success and blessing flow naturally from right action.

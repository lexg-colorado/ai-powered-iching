# Hexagram 12 — ䷋ 否 (pǐ) — Standstill

**Alternate Names:** "Standstill", "Stagnation", "Obstruction", "Selfish Persons", "Separation"

**Upper Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)
**Lower Trigram:** ☷ 坤 (kūn) — Earth (地)

**King Wen Number:** 12 | **Binary:** 111000

---

## Overview

Obstruction represents a time when heaven and earth are separated, when those above and below are in conflict. Small and petty forces gain temporary dominance, blocking progress and creating general disharmony. This hexagram counsels acceptance of limitation and inner consolidation.

## Judgment

Standstill occurs when virtue withdraws and small-mindedness prevails, creating mutual disconnection. Progress is impossible during this period; the wise withdraw from public endeavor and focus on inner cultivation. Patience and integrity preserved during hardship ensure eventual return of favorable conditions.

## Image

Heaven moving upward and earth settling downward represent forces in natural separation. Like diverging paths, the way forward closes; movement becomes futile and stillness becomes the appropriate response.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The first line experiences the beginning of standstill, where obstruction first appears. Withdrawal is possible without loss; recognizing the change early and stepping back prevents being trapped by advancing difficulty.

### Six in the Second Place

The second line finds itself caught in developing obstruction. Difficulty deepens but inner resources remain intact; accepting limitation without despair preserves spirit for the long period ahead.

### Six in the Third Place

The third line reaches the threshold of acute difficulty. Continued effort produces no result; the wise cease struggling and accept circumstances, allowing apparent failure to become preparation for future opportunity.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The fourth line shows transition from passive suffering to active acceptance. Approaching authority with humility despite blockage creates possibility of alliance; sincere effort toward those in power may find response.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The fifth line holds central position amid general chaos. Even from authority's seat, one cannot force change; accepting this limitation and maintaining righteousness demonstrates the dignity that eventually attracts support.

### Nine at the Top

The sixth line shows standstill ending, the blockage lifting. Withdrawal gives way to returning possibility; those who maintained integrity during hardship now reap the benefit of sustained inner quality.

# Hexagram 35 — ䷢ 晉 (jìn) — Progress

**Alternate Names:** "Progress", "Prospering", "Advancement", "Promotion", "Sunrise"

**Upper Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)
**Lower Trigram:** ☷ 坤 (kūn) — Earth (地)

**King Wen Number:** 35 | **Binary:** 101000

---

## Overview

Prospering signifies the bright ascent of favorable circumstances and the advancement of worthy endeavors. Like the sun rising over the earth, clarity and growth spread naturally. This is a season of expansion when effort meets with receptive conditions, enabling steady progress toward meaningful goals.

## Judgment

Prospering brings continuous advantage when pursued with sincerity. The superior person advances through virtue, attracting benefit as naturally as light spreads. Favor flows not from force but from correct alignment with the times and receptiveness to opportunity.

## Image

Fire rises above earth, spreading warmth and illumination in all directions. This symbolizes brightness expanding, virtue becoming visible, and natural growth proceeding without obstruction.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

Sincere progress attracts support from those who recognize genuine virtue. The beginning of advancement often appears modest, yet attracts patronage and opportunity. True merit draws recognition without boastfulness or force.

### Six in the Second Place

Progress tempered by anxiety brings small regret but no lasting blame. Doubt and worry may attend advancement, yet continued sincerity gradually dispels concern. The path of growth may not always feel secure, yet careful progress yields good result.

### Six in the Third Place

Universal agreement in advancement brings certainty of success. When all recognize the virtue and purpose of one's progress, obstacles dissolve and support accumulates. This phase brings considerable good fortune through widely acknowledged merit.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

Progress achieved through proper relationship with superior authority brings great fortune. Recognition and support from those in position to grant advancement opens many paths. Yet such favor rests on genuine worth and continued virtue.

### Six in the Fifth Place

Regret eventually dissipates when one advances in accordance with righteous principles. Initial setbacks or small losses may attend progress, but the overall movement remains favorable. Persisting with integrity transforms small sorrows into eventual gain.

### Nine at the Top

Blind advancement pursued without discernment invites conflict and reversal. Aggressive, heedless progress that disregards others brings resistance and misfortune. The superior person advances only in harmony with the greater order of things.

# Hexagram 3 — ䷂ 屯 (zhūn) — Difficulty at the Beginning

**Alternate Names:** "Difficulty at the Beginning", "Gathering Support", "Hoarding", "Sprouting", "Accumulation"

**Upper Trigram:** ☵ 坎 (kǎn) — Water (水)
**Lower Trigram:** ☳ 震 (zhèn) — Thunder (雷)

**King Wen Number:** 3 | **Binary:** 010001

---

## Overview

Hexagram marking the turbulent emergence of form from undifferentiated potential. Water and thunder intermingle at the threshold of manifestation, creating chaos and energy. Represents the gathering of resources, the difficulty of beginning, and the necessity of organization amid confusion.

## Judgment

Difficulty at the Beginning brings supreme success through constancy and proper order. Gather helpers and establish structure despite obstacles; advance with deliberate steps rather than hasty force. Organization and loyalty prove essential to navigate the initial chaos.

## Image

Thunder beneath water conveys hidden power stirring, turbulence concealed yet rising. The superior person gathers purpose and accumulates resources carefully, bringing order to confusion.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

Hesitation and difficulty—beneficial to establish helpers and gain support. At the outset, confusion reigns; seek worthy companions and advisors to clarify direction and share the burden.

### Six in the Second Place

Encumbered and uncertain, like a carriage stuck—stop and consult. Entanglement marks this inner position; patience and counsel from above are required to unstick progress.

### Six in the Third Place

Hunting in the field yields no advantage—the way is not yet clear. Confusion persists at this transitional threshold; action becomes premature and scattered. Better to wait for clarity before committing force.

### Six in the Fourth Place

Seeking alliance through marriage—moving toward union. Entering the outer realm, one seeks partnership and support from higher authority. Readiness to serve and align with greater purpose opens paths forward.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

Difficulty yields small gains—constancy brings light. The central position struggles against resistance, yet modest progress emerges through steadfast effort. Continue with patience; breakthroughs follow constancy.

### Six at the Top

A weeping child—the gathered intention breaks. Extreme confusion and loss of direction characterize this culmination. Abandon the effort; begin again with greater clarity and firmer foundation.

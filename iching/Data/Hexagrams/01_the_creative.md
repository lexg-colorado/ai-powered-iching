# Hexagram 1 — ䷀ 乾 (qián) — The Creative

**Alternate Names:** "The Creative", "Strong Action", "The Key", "Celestial Force", "Pure Yang"

**Upper Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)
**Lower Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)

**King Wen Number:** 1 | **Binary:** 111111

---

## Overview

Hexagram of pure creative power and perpetual motion. Represents the primordial force of heaven, expressing ultimate yang energy in its fullest manifestation. Symbolizes leadership, strength, and the initiating impulse of all things.

## Judgment

The Creative brings supreme success through persistent effort and correct alignment. Those who embody its qualities achieve their aims through unwavering determination and natural authority. Advance with confidence, for the path is clear and supported by cosmic force.

## Image

Heaven in motion above heaven conveys unceasing movement and perpetual activity. The superior person draws inspiration from this constancy, cultivating strength and renewing effort without cessation.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The hidden dragon at rest symbolizes dormant potential and the virtue of restraint. One should remain still and observe, gathering strength before action commences.

### Nine in the Second Place

The dragon appears in the field—favorable to see the great person. This marks the emergence from obscurity, a time to present oneself and establish one's presence in the world.

### Nine in the Third Place

The superior person remains active all day, vigilant at nightfall, aware of danger. Ceaseless effort and mindfulness prevent complacency and ensure sustained progress.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The dragon springs from the abyss—whether ascending or descending, no blame attends either path. A critical juncture where circumstance determines the course; readiness and flexibility are essential.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The flying dragon reaches heaven—beneficial to see the great person. This position of rulership brings recognition and triumph, yet warns against arrogance or abandonment of principle.

### Nine at the Top

The arrogant dragon regrets its position, reaching too far. Excess and pride have overextended the creative force; humility and retreat become necessary to restore equilibrium.

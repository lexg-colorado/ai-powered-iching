The **I Ching** represents each of its **64 hexagrams** as a stack of **six lines**. Each line can be:

- **Yang** (solid line) → often mapped to **1**
    
- **Yin** (broken line) → often mapped to **0**
    

If we treat each hexagram like a **6-bit binary number**, we can encode them from **000000 to 111111**. Conventionally, the **bottom line is the least significant bit (LSB)** and the **top line is the most significant bit (MSB)**.

Below is the complete binary sequence of the 64 possible hexagrams.

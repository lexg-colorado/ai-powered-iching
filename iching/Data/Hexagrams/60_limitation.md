# Hexagram 60 — ䷻ 節 (jié) — Limitation

**Alternate Names:** "Limitation", "Articulating", "Moderation", "Temperance", "Thrift"

**Upper Trigram:** ☵ 坎 (kǎn) — Water (水)
**Lower Trigram:** ☱ 兌 (duì) — Lake (澤)

**King Wen Number:** 60 | **Binary:** 010011

---

## Overview

Water over Lake represents appropriate boundaries and the rhythm of natural limits. Just as nodes articulate a bamboo stalk, this hexagram shows how constraints create structure and beauty. Limitation properly observed brings freedom and prevents excess from breeding suffering.

## Judgment

Progress depends upon establishing just limits that honor both inner principle and outer circumstance. Strict moderation creates conditions for sustained harmony. The superior person uses restraint not from fear but from understanding that all things flourish within proper bounds.

## Image

The lake contains water at its proper level. The superior person thereby establishes measures and determines ritual propriety.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

Simple, natural limits set early prevent later suffering. The beginner who accepts modest boundaries discovers they are not oppressive but liberating. One line of demarcation preserves freedom everywhere else.

### Nine in the Second Place

When one resists necessary restraint, hardship follows. Yet excessive limitation becomes its own prison. The balance is to accept constraints that serve the greater purpose willingly.

### Six in the Third Place

The center contains strength within proper bounds. One neither chafes against limitations nor becomes weakened by them. This stable midpoint allows consistent forward progress.

### Six in the Fourth Place

Those at ease with appropriate limits influence others toward balance. Resignation to constraint is not required; clarity about its benefit brings grace. Articulation creates order without resentment.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

As conditions require tighter discipline, the superior person follows willingly. The ability to adjust limits as circumstances demand prevents breakdown. Flexibility within firmness is the way.

### Six at the Top

When extreme limitation chokes off life and progress, it becomes tyranny. Breaking through excessive constraint is not excess but necessary correction. Even discipline must serve life, not extinguish it.

# Hexagram 51 — ䷲ 震 (zhèn) — The Arousing

**Alternate Names:** "The Arousing", "Shake", "Shock", "Thunder"

**Upper Trigram:** ☳ 震 (zhèn) — Thunder (雷)
**Lower Trigram:** ☳ 震 (zhèn) — Thunder (雷)

**King Wen Number:** 51 | **Binary:** 001001

---

## Overview

Thunder represents sudden awakening and the force that shakes complacency into motion. The rumbling call stirs what was dormant and brings startling clarity. This hexagram embodies the beneficial shock that precedes transformation and growth.

## Judgment

When thunder sounds, all things tremble and then settle into new configuration. The shock is neither inherently good nor bad—its value lies in the response of those who hear it. The superior person is stirred but not overcome, learning from the tremor what was previously hidden.

## Image

Thunder repeating upon itself carries power and accumulation. The superior person thus trembles with reverence and conducts herself with careful attention to duty.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

Thunder startles the one unaware, bringing sudden fear. Yet if this shock leads to proper vigilance and renewed attention to what matters, the initial tremor becomes a blessing.

### Six in the Second Place

Danger approaches from the arousing shock. One should move swiftly but thoughtfully, neither frozen by fear nor reckless in response, maintaining inner calm amid outer turbulence.

### Six in the Third Place

The shock moves through and one remains standing. When the tremor passes, assess what has shifted and gather strength for what comes next without clinging to what was shaken loose.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

Successive shocks test resolve. Each wave of disturbance reveals character; those who remain centered despite repeated shocks demonstrate their capacity to weather transformation.

### Six in the Fifth Place

Thunder rises and falls, leaving all scattered. Though the force disperses one's plans, this clearing may reveal what was needed more than what was lost.

### Six at the Top

The shock reaches its limit and settles into silence. Having weathered the arousing force, one emerges clarified and reoriented, ready to act from this new vantage point.

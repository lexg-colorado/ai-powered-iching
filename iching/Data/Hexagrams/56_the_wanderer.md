# Hexagram 56 — ䷷ 旅 (lǚ) — The Wanderer

**Alternate Names:** "The Wanderer", "Sojourning", "Traveling", "The Stranger"

**Upper Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)
**Lower Trigram:** ☶ 艮 (gèn) — Mountain (山)

**King Wen Number:** 56 | **Binary:** 101100

---

## Overview

The Wanderer embodies the condition of being away from home, living among strangers in transit. Though displacement brings vulnerability, it also offers freedom from convention and opportunity for observation. This hexagram speaks to the journey itself as teacher and transformation.

## Judgment

The wanderer finds fortune through maintaining modesty and proper conduct. Far from home, one is noticed and judged by every action; integrity becomes both shield and key. The journey itself, though uncertain, holds value beyond any single destination.

## Image

Fire atop Mountain suggests brightness amid stillness, illumination from movement. The superior person thus remains clear in principle though moving through uncertain terrain, letting her inner clarity guide external conduct.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The wanderer departs in humble manner, careful in small things. Begin the journey properly by attending to details and maintaining respect for those encountered; a good start builds foundation for safe passage.

### Six in the Second Place

The wanderer arrives at shelter and finds the inn. Rest is found through proper demeanor and modest conduct; the stranger who behaves with courtesy finds welcome even in unfamiliar lands.

### Nine in the Third Place

The inn burns and the lodging is lost; the wanderer stands exposed. Misfortune arrives through neglecting proper conduct or trusting too freely. Recover by moving on with lessons learned rather than dwelling on loss.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The wanderer journeys onward and attains shelter, though the heart remains unsettled. Find temporary safety but do not mistake rest for arrival; the journey continues and teaches through its very incompleteness.

### Six in the Fifth Place

The arrow finds its mark and honors come through valorous conduct. Distinction arrives when one is noticed by those of quality for genuine worth displayed during the journey.

### Nine at the Top

The wanderer reaches a village and rests secure, having completed the passage. The journey's end finds the traveler transformed by what was witnessed and endured; wisdom gained through sojourning becomes lasting treasure.

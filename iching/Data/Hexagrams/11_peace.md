# Hexagram 11 — ䷊ 泰 (tài) — Peace

**Alternate Names:** "Peace", "Smooth Going", "Greatness", "Prosperity", "Free Passage"

**Upper Trigram:** ☷ 坤 (kūn) — Earth (地)
**Lower Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)

**King Wen Number:** 11 | **Binary:** 000111

---

## Overview

Pervading represents a time of harmony and favorable conditions where heaven and earth work together. This hexagram shows the natural flow of blessing through proper alignment of forces. Prosperity comes not from struggle but from receptivity to favorable circumstances.

## Judgment

Peace arises when those above are receptive and those below contribute freely, creating mutual benefit. This propitious time supports growth and expansion across all endeavors. The wise recognize this as a temporary alignment and prepare for inevitable change.

## Image

Earth resting above heaven represents an inversion of natural order that paradoxically brings peace through perfect adaptation. The boundaries dissolve into natural flow, and movement occurs without resistance or force.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The first line enters the time of peace from previous difficulty. Caution remains wise despite changing conditions; prudent restraint preserves gains and allows proper rooting of new growth.

### Nine in the Second Place

The second line rises with peaceful conditions fully established. This inner position enjoys safety and sincere connection; aligned with others, one may act with confidence tempered by continued virtue.

### Nine in the Third Place

The third line approaches the transition point where peace reaches its limit. Awareness of impermanence becomes necessary; excessive attachment to current conditions breeds carelessness when inevitably change approaches.

### Six in the Fourth Place

The fourth line moves between inner security and outer position. Crossing from favorable to uncertain territory requires shedding excessive reliance on current conditions; authentic confidence comes from inner quality rather than external advantage.

### Six in the Fifth Place

The fifth line holds central authority during peaceful conditions. Leadership demonstrates genuine benefit to all, gaining trust through generous sharing of prosperity; this position consolidates peace through continued righteousness.

### Six at the Top

The sixth line shows peace approaching its natural end. Excessive prolongation of favorable conditions invites reversal; the wise recognize completion and prepare for necessary transition without regret.

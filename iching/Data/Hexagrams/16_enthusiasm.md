# Hexagram 16 — ䷏ 豫 (yù) — Enthusiasm

**Alternate Names:** "Enthusiasm", "Providing-For", "Excess", "Comfort", "Preparedness"

**Upper Trigram:** ☳ 震 (zhèn) — Thunder (雷)
**Lower Trigram:** ☷ 坤 (kūn) — Earth (地)

**King Wen Number:** 16 | **Binary:** 001000

---

## Overview

Providing-For represents joyful readiness and the enthusiasm that mobilizes action. Like thunder rising from the earth, this hexagram shows the power of inspired preparation and eager participation. Success comes through responding to genuine inspiration while maintaining awareness of limits.

## Judgment

Enthusiasm creates natural motivation and willing cooperation among those gathered. The wise leader channels this energy toward worthy purpose and knows when inspiration must transform into discipline. Excessive indulgence in comfort or delay of action transforms enthusiasm into liability.

## Image

Thunder emerging from the earth represents the surge of energy and awakening readiness. Enthusiasm works through natural arousal of will and eager anticipation; this power properly directed accomplishes much, but undirected becomes mere excitement.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The first line experiences initial enthusiasm and eagerness for action. Young energy bursts forth without full judgment; the wise channel this vitality carefully, allowing it to develop naturally while applying gentle guidance.

### Six in the Second Place

The second line shows developed enthusiasm rooted in proper understanding. Inner alignment brings satisfaction and credibility; one gains trust and willing followers because genuine conviction rather than pretense motivates action.

### Six in the Third Place

The third line reaches the point where enthusiasm must be examined and controlled. The danger of excessive indulgence appears; the wise distinguish between beneficial enthusiasm and mere entertainment, refocusing energy toward true purpose.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The fourth line moves from inner enthusiasm toward position of shared responsibility. Communicating genuine vision inspires others; this position requires integrity because false enthusiasm rapidly reveals itself and destroys credibility.

### Six in the Fifth Place

The fifth line holds central authority, embodying and directing collective enthusiasm. The ruler who maintains genuine vision amid crowd excitement guides well; those inspired by authentic purpose accomplish extraordinary things.

### Six at the Top

The sixth line shows enthusiasm reaching completion or excess, where initial energy dissipates. The work either stands completed or the moment has passed; clinging to past enthusiasm brings only weariness. Transition to new beginning requires releasing former inspiration.

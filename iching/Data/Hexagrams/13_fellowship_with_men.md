# Hexagram 13 — ䷌ 同人 (tóng rén) — Fellowship with Men

**Alternate Names:** "Fellowship with Men", "Gathering Men", "Community", "Concording People", "Human Connection"

**Upper Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)
**Lower Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)

**King Wen Number:** 13 | **Binary:** 111101

---

## Overview

Concording People represents unity based on shared purpose rather than imposed control. Like fire rising toward heaven, this hexagram shows the natural gathering of those who recognize common interest. True fellowship transcends position and kinship, bound by genuine alignment of values.

## Judgment

Fellowship succeeds when based on sincerity and openness across traditional boundaries. Great fortune comes to those who gather like-minded individuals toward worthy purpose. The path requires transparency and removal of private agenda; narrow interests must yield to common good.

## Image

Fire rising toward heaven shows warmth spreading widely, drawing all things toward its light. Fellowship works through natural attraction rather than coercion, each drawn by the perceived good of the gathering.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The first line begins fellowship in simplicity, where kinship with nearby others is natural. Honest direct connection requires no pretense; this foundation ensures that deeper bonds build on genuine connection rather than false appearance.

### Six in the Second Place

The second line finds itself excluded from immediate fellowship, separated by circumstance or position. Patient integrity alone keeps the heart aligned; this solitary waiting preserves sincerity and eventually calls forth recognition from afar.

### Nine in the Third Place

The third line reaches the critical point where true fellowship faces hidden obstacle. Suspicion and rivalry may lurk within the group; the wise remain alert to shadows within alliance and address them directly.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The fourth line breaks the bounds of limited fellowship, reaching toward wider community. This expansion requires releasing exclusive attachments; genuine vision that includes all creates unexpectedly powerful alliance.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The fifth line holds central position in established fellowship. Authority here flows naturally from recognized virtue; true leadership gathers willing followers without command and directs collective will toward worthy ends.

### Nine at the Top

The sixth line shows fellowship approaching its limit or transformation. Perfect unity dissolves as the group grows too large; the wise release the form and allow the spirit of fellowship to manifest in new ways.

# Hexagram 29 — ䷜ 坎 (kǎn) — The Abysmal

**Alternate Names:** "The Abysmal", "The Gorge", "Repeated Danger", "The Deep", "The Abyss"

**Upper Trigram:** ☵ 坎 (kǎn) — Water (水)
**Lower Trigram:** ☵ 坎 (kǎn) — Water (水)

**King Wen Number:** 29 | **Binary:** 010010

---

## Overview

Water doubled reveals the nature of danger itself—what is deep, dark, and perilous. Yet water does not stagnate; it flows continuously through all obstacles, seeking the lowest place without complaint. This hexagram represents danger that cannot be avoided, and the wisdom of moving through it with integrity rather than resistance.

## Judgment

Danger repeated tests sincerity and reveals true character. The superior person maintains inner truth even when surrounded by peril, flowing like water around obstacles while losing nothing of essence. Success comes not from escape but from perseverance through the abyss.

## Image

Water flows downward ceaselessly, penetrating all barriers without hardening. The image teaches that gentle persistence accomplishes what force cannot; the way through danger is not to fight it but to move with it.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The first line, yielding at the base, shows one entering danger for the first time. Fear is natural, yet one who enters with caution may traverse safely. This is the position of the beginner in the abyss.

### Nine in the Second Place

The second line, firm and central, indicates one in the midst of danger with inner stability. Though surrounded by peril, the sincere heart remains unmoved. This position brings advancement through truthfulness.

### Six in the Third Place

The third line, yielding and unstable, depicts one advancing recklessly into danger. Each approach brings only repetition of the same peril. One must stop and reconsider strategy rather than pushing forward blindly.

### Six in the Fourth Place

The fourth line, firm and approaching safety, shows one who nearly exits the danger. A small cup suffices where grand vessels fail; simplicity aids escape. This position teaches that modesty aids deliverance.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The fifth line, yielding in authority, represents one through whom others pass. The danger does not overwhelm; it flows through in its proper channel. This is the position of the guide through the abyss.

### Six at the Top

The sixth line, yielding at the extremity, shows one bound and trapped in the deepest part. Stiffness of thought has created this prison; release comes only through surrendering all resistance. This position teaches ultimate surrender as liberation.

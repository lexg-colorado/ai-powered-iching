# Hexagram 45 — ䷬ 萃 (cuì) — Gathering Together

**Alternate Names:** "Gathering Together", "Clustering", "Assembly", "Massing"

**Upper Trigram:** ☱ 兌 (duì) — Lake (澤)
**Lower Trigram:** ☷ 坤 (kūn) — Earth (地)

**King Wen Number:** 45 | **Binary:** 011000

---

## Overview

Clustering represents the natural congregation of similar elements and the consolidation of scattered forces. The lake rests upon the earth, collecting what flows downward. This hexagram favors unity and the harmonious assembly of people and resources.

## Judgment

Gathering Together brings good fortune when those of like mind unite with sincerity. The superior organizes the dispersed and strengthens community through clarity of purpose and fair dealing with all.

## Image

The lake sinks into the earth and gathers its waters in low places. This natural concentration of what has been scattered symbolizes the drawing together of energies and intentions.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The first line describes sadness and uncertainty when the gathering process begins. Initial assembly is often confused; clarity emerges only as individual elements commit fully to the collective purpose.

### Six in the Second Place

The second line speaks of sincere intention that draws others toward unity. When one's heart is genuinely devoted to the common good, even those who are hesitant will join the gathering.

### Six in the Third Place

The third line warns against excessive self-assertion within the collective. The gathered whole is stronger than any individual; those who insist on prominence rather than service create discord.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The fourth line addresses the gathering under high and capable leadership. When the superior organizes with wisdom, those who might otherwise be scattered find direction and grow stronger together.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The fifth line emphasizes the position of authority in managing the assembly. The leader's good fortune depends on sincerity and equity; showing favor without fairness causes resentment and undermines unity.

### Six at the Top

The sixth line describes the poignant moment when gathering is abandoned or impossible. Even tears shed for what cannot be united carry their own dignity; one must accept limitation and move forward with what remains.

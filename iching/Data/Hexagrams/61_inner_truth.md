# Hexagram 61 — ䷼ 中孚 (zhōng fú) — Inner Truth

**Alternate Names:** "Inner Truth", "Center Returning", "Central Sincerity", "Innermost Sincerity", "Faithfulness"

**Upper Trigram:** ☴ 巽 (xùn) — Wind (風)
**Lower Trigram:** ☱ 兌 (duì) — Lake (澤)

**King Wen Number:** 61 | **Binary:** 110011

---

## Overview

Wind over Lake represents sincerity at the innermost core, creating authentic connection and mutual trust. This hexagram shows that truth emanating from genuine inner conviction moves hearts and accomplishes great things. When the center is honest, outer forms naturally align.

## Judgment

Success flows from abiding in truth without pretense or compromise. Even the smallest creatures respond when one acts from sincere center. This hexagram teaches that unfeigned sincerity possesses transformative power over obstacles and opposition.

## Image

The lake receives the wind within it. The superior person thereby discerns criminal intent and pronounces judgment with fairness.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

Inner truth naturally expresses itself without effort. One need not announce sincerity; it shines forth in action. The first step is to ensure one's own center is absolutely honest.

### Nine in the Second Place

When inner sincerity meets doubt from others, patience proves its case. Constancy in honest conduct gradually wins over skepticism. True motives eventually become apparent even to the suspicious.

### Six in the Third Place

Within proper community, sincerity flowers. Alliance with those of like heart multiplies one's influence. Yet this is not conformity but authentic gathering of aligned principles.

### Six in the Fourth Place

With sincerity as foundation, one reaches even those far distant or opposed. Hearts speak to hearts across barriers of circumstance. The truthful person becomes a bridge between divided camps.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

Bonds created through sincerity are strong and enduring. Those connected in genuine principle remain loyal even when external circumstances shift. This is the highest form of relationship.

### Nine at the Top

When one's sincerity is questioned or rejected, the inner truth remains unshaken. Continuing in honest conduct despite criticism proves the genuineness of conviction. The final fruit is vindication through steadfast integrity.

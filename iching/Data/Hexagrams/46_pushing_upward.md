# Hexagram 46 — ䷭ 升 (shēng) — Pushing Upward

**Alternate Names:** "Pushing Upward", "Ascending", "Growing Upward", "Promotion"

**Upper Trigram:** ☷ 坤 (kūn) — Earth (地)
**Lower Trigram:** ☴ 巽 (xùn) — Wind (風)

**King Wen Number:** 46 | **Binary:** 000110

---

## Overview

Ascending represents steady progress and the gradual elevation of influence and capability. The wood beneath the earth pushes upward through nourishing soil, rising step by step toward light. This hexagram favors patient development and recognition of growth.

## Judgment

Pushing Upward brings supreme good fortune, especially when pursued with sincerity and without force. The superior advances through service and cultivation; the way is open to rise through the natural unfolding of circumstance.

## Image

Wood grows upward from the earth that nourishes it; the ascent is gradual, rooted, and inevitable. This steady mounting of influence symbolizes progress that is organic and sustainable rather than sudden.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The first line describes the beginning of ascent when confidence is still forming. The first steps are humble and require consistent effort; one must gather strength and resource before attempting greater heights.

### Nine in the Second Place

The second line speaks of the blessing when superiors take notice of genuine merit. Advancement comes through being worthy of attention; those who perform their duties well without arrogance attract recognition.

### Nine in the Third Place

The third line emphasizes the importance of moving into new positions without resistance or complaint. When opportunity for advancement appears, seizing it with grace and readiness ensures smooth transition to greater responsibility.

### Six in the Fourth Place

The fourth line warns against the pride and haste that can accompany rising fortune. Even as one ascends, maintaining humility and caution prevents the missteps that topple the newly elevated.

### Six in the Fifth Place

The fifth line describes the supreme good fortune of those who advance steadily into positions of authority. The one who has risen through merit and service naturally gains the trust and loyalty of those below.

### Six at the Top

The sixth line cautions that continued upward striving beyond one's proper station brings loss. At the height of ascent, the wise consolidate their position and turn attention to stabilizing what they have gained.

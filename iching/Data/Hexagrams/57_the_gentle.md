# Hexagram 57 — ䷸ 巽 (xùn) — The Gentle

**Alternate Names:** "The Gentle", "Ground", "Wind", "Penetrating", "The Submissive"

**Upper Trigram:** ☴ 巽 (xùn) — Wind (風)
**Lower Trigram:** ☴ 巽 (xùn) — Wind (風)

**King Wen Number:** 57 | **Binary:** 110110

---

## Overview

Hexagram of the Wind principle applied twice, representing gentleness and penetrating influence. The doubled Wind trigram emphasizes subtle, continuous action and the power of gradual persuasion. This hexagram teaches that persistent, gentle pressure accomplishes what force cannot.

## Judgment

Success comes through yielding and gentle persistence rather than forceful action. The time calls for adaptation and allowing circumstances to unfold naturally while maintaining steady inner principle. Progress is achieved through flexibility and consistent, subtle influence.

## Image

Wind moving across wind creates an image of penetrating action without resistance. The superior person thereby spreads instruction and carries through the affairs of the people.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The beginning is tentative, like wind awakening. Moving forward with gentle resolve builds momentum. One must not overthink but proceed with careful attention.

### Nine in the Second Place

When the gentle becomes indecisive, doubt clouds the path. Here command is needed with clarity, though the voice remains soft. Strength of principle guides even uncertain action.

### Nine in the Third Place

The center moves with moderate force and natural timing. Guidance comes from inner conviction rather than external pressure. This position allows the gentle way to penetrate most effectively.

### Six in the Fourth Place

Hesitation and second thoughts create obstacles. One must commit fully to the gentle approach without wavering. Penetration requires continuity; starting and stopping defeats the purpose.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The approach reaches those resistant to change. Persistence and good faith slowly transform even rigid positions. Progress appears small but is genuine and lasting.

### Nine at the Top

When excess gentleness becomes self-surrender, the way reverses into harm. The superior person knows when softness must give way to clear boundaries. Penetration should serve purpose, not become formless diffusion.

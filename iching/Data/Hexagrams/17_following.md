# Hexagram 17 — ䷐ 隨 (suí) — Following

**Alternate Names:** "Following", "Pursuit", "Adapting", "Compliance"

**Upper Trigram:** ☱ 兌 (duì) — Lake (澤)
**Lower Trigram:** ☳ 震 (zhèn) — Thunder (雷)

**King Wen Number:** 17 | **Binary:** 011001

---

## Overview

Following represents the natural movement of entities in harmony with prevailing currents and forces. It embodies the principle of adaptive response—not blind obedience, but intelligent attunement to circumstances and the intentions of worthy guides.

## Judgment

Following brings fortune when pursued with genuine discernment. The wise follow those of superior character and wisdom, while poor followers abandon their principles. The timing of when to follow and when to lead determines success.

## Image

Thunder beneath the Lake suggests movement arising from stillness, and water flowing downward. The superior person retires to rest and preserve their energy, following the rhythms of nature and season.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

At the foundation, one may be bound by attachment to following others without examining their worth. Discernment of character precedes commitment to any path.

### Six in the Second Place

Following a lesser person or false guide leads toward entanglement. The superior person maintains inner autonomy even while externally compliant to circumstance.

### Six in the Third Place

Loyalty to worthy guidance produces steadiness and recognition. Yet the follower must remain alert to changes in condition that demand new responses.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

Following without discrimination dilutes one's own authority and judgment. When following ceases to serve the greater good, reclamation of independent action becomes necessary.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

Following with sincere trust and genuine commitment draws fortune and approval. The wholehearted adherent gains support because integrity speaks clearly.

### Six at the Top

At the culmination of following, one discovers that authentic adherence transcends mere compliance—it becomes conscious participation in a shared purpose.

# Hexagram 8 — ䷇ 比 (bǐ) — Holding Together

**Alternate Names:** "Holding Together", "Alliance", "Union", "Seeking Union", "Companionship"

**Upper Trigram:** ☵ 坎 (kǎn) — Water (水)
**Lower Trigram:** ☷ 坤 (kūn) — Earth (地)

**King Wen Number:** 8 | **Binary:** 010000

---

## Overview

Hexagram of union and organized togetherness. Water above earth suggests moisture nourishing the ground, the natural affinity of complementary forces. Represents alliance, grouping, and the bonds of loyalty that hold societies and relationships intact.

## Judgment

Holding Together brings supreme success; sincere union among the worthy creates unbreakable bonds. Examine the foundations of allegiance; those who join late may find themselves excluded. Constancy through established relationships yields lasting benefit.

## Image

Water upon the earth flows to nurture and hold all things. The superior person cultivates affinities, gathering kindred spirits and establishing bonds of lasting loyalty.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

Sincerity within holds together—no blame, it fills the vessel. The foundation of true union rests upon genuine integrity and heartfelt commitment. Those with honest intention attract devoted companions.

### Six in the Second Place

Inner holding brings constancy—advantageous to persist. The inner circle of closest allies provides stability; loyalty to those near brings lasting success. This position strengthens through devoted dedication.

### Six in the Third Place

You seek union with those above—regret. The transition outward from inner circle may scatter focus or create entanglement with those of unequal worth. Beware of ascending beyond one's proper station.

### Six in the Fourth Place

External seeking toward the worthy brings fortune—sincerity gathers from the distance. The outer position reaches upward to authority; those of genuine virtue attract higher support and allies beyond immediate proximity.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

Mutual holding at the center—supreme fortune through constancy. The ruler's position manifests perfect union; all gather naturally around centered, virtuous authority. This is the place of magnetic attraction and complete accord.

### Six at the Top

No head for holding together—misfortune. The extreme position lacks leadership or direction; the group fragments without central authority. Dissolution follows when coherence and guidance disappear.

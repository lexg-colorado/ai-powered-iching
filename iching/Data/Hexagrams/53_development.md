# Hexagram 53 — ䷴ 漸 (jiàn) — Development

**Alternate Names:** "Development", "Gradual Progress", "Infiltrating", "Advancement"

**Upper Trigram:** ☴ 巽 (xùn) — Wind (風)
**Lower Trigram:** ☶ 艮 (gèn) — Mountain (山)

**King Wen Number:** 53 | **Binary:** 110100

---

## Overview

Development emphasizes measured progress through persistent small steps. Like water gradually infiltrating stone or a tree spreading its roots downward while growing upward, advancement comes through consistency and proper sequence. This hexagram favors the patient accumulation of change.

## Judgment

Development finds favor through steady application over time. The wise one does not leap but advances position by position, establishing each level securely before moving higher. This measured pace ensures lasting achievement.

## Image

Wind rises through the Wood, gradual ascent through what supports growth. The superior person thus conducts herself with propriety and refines her virtue through sustained, step-by-step cultivation.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The wild goose lands by the shore, finding small advantage in new territory. Begin cautiously, establishing footing in unfamiliar ground before advancing further.

### Six in the Second Place

The wild goose rests on stone in the glen, eating and drinking in contentment. Find stability and sustenance where you are; progress comes through making full use of present circumstance.

### Nine in the Third Place

The wild goose advances to the high plain where danger lessens. Press forward when conditions permit, but remain alert to shifts in terrain and whether continued advancement serves your purpose.

### Six in the Fourth Place

The wild goose rests in the tree, finding a safe perch above danger. Secure your position at a higher level, though remember that rest in safety is still temporary; continued development awaits.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The wild goose approaches the summit but cannot yet reach it. Recognize the limits of present ability and circumstance; prepare for the final stage without forcing prema-ture ascent.

### Nine at the Top

The wild goose reaches the summit and settles into her place. Having advanced through all stages with patience and propriety, success is complete; now maintain this height with wisdom.

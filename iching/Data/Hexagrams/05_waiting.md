# Hexagram 5 — ䷄ 需 (xū) — Waiting

**Alternate Names:** "Waiting", "Moistened", "Arriving", "Nourishment", "Attending"

**Upper Trigram:** ☵ 坎 (kǎn) — Water (水)
**Lower Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)

**King Wen Number:** 5 | **Binary:** 010111

---

## Overview

Hexagram of necessary patience and sustenance amid uncertainty. Water suspended above heaven suggests the gathering clouds before rain, the nourishment ready to fall. Represents waiting with confidence, the virtue of readiness, and trust in timely provision.

## Judgment

Waiting brings supreme success through faith and sincere perseverance. Trust in heaven's timing; the nourishment comes to those who remain centered and do not chase after prematurely. Steadfast readiness meets the approaching provision.

## Image

Clouds suspended above heaven convey moisture gathering, the promise of nourishment delayed. The superior person prepares nourishment and attends to orderly sustenance, remaining patient for the appointed hour.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

Waiting at the frontier—return brings no blame. The first stage of waiting is remote and preparatory; one remains at the threshold, alert but not advancing. Patience at the boundary promises no regret.

### Nine in the Second Place

Waiting on sand—small discourse brings enduring constancy. Neither in the deep water nor on firm ground, this middle position permits gradual approach. Humble conversation and persistent effort yield results.

### Nine in the Third Place

Waiting in mud courts danger—spirits arrive to assist. Encroachment into murkiness brings peril, yet unexpected help emerges from subtle sources. Caution paired with openness to unseen aid proves wise.

### Six in the Fourth Place

Waiting in blood—issuing from the cave brings constancy. A critical juncture where old conflicts must be resolved; emerging from past darkness with steady purpose avoids further entanglement.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

Nourishment by wine and food—constancy brings fortune. The central position enjoys abundance and proper sustenance; the way has become comfortable and assured. Maintain steadfastness to preserve the gains.

### Six at the Top

Entering the cave—three unexpected guests arrive. Retreat and closure prove perilous, yet unforeseen aid arrives despite withdrawal. Better to remain accessible; fortune comes through openness, not isolation.

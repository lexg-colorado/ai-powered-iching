# Hexagram 22 — ䷕ 賁 (bì) — Grace

**Alternate Names:** "Grace", "Adorning", "Elegance", "Beauty", "Adornment"

**Upper Trigram:** ☶ 艮 (gèn) — Mountain (山)
**Lower Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)

**King Wen Number:** 22 | **Binary:** 100101

---

## Overview

Adorning represents the principle of refinement and the enhancement of form through subtle beautification. It embodies not superficial decoration but the disciplined arrangement of elements according to inherent principles, where inner quality finds outward expression through cultivated aesthetics.

## Judgment

Adorning brings fortune in matters requiring careful presentation and refined execution. Though not fundamental to the most vital concerns, graceful form supports the transmission of worthy substance and earns recognition through its elegance.

## Image

Fire adorning the Mountain creates brilliance and visible distinction. The superior person brings clarity to practical matters through disciplined attention to form, ensuring that function is neither compromised nor obscured.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

At the foundation, simple adornment of the feet or lower aspects suggests modest beautification of minor matters. Beginning refinement establishes foundation for more elaborate grace.

### Six in the Second Place

Adorning the beard or facial hair indicates beautification of the expression and presence. Personal grooming and dignified appearance command recognition and respect from those encountered.

### Nine in the Third Place

Refined adornment of the entire person brings lasting favor and steady good fortune. Comprehensive elegance in bearing and presentation generates attraction and positive regard.

### Six in the Fourth Place

At the center, one may encounter the choice between genuine refinement and false decoration. Simplicity and honesty of presentation may serve better than elaborate embellishment.

### Six in the Fifth Place

At the fifth line, grace adorns substantial accomplishment and renders recognition certain. When inner worth meets refined expression, admiration flows from those of discernment.

### Nine at the Top

At culmination, excessive adornment obscures substance and becomes counterproductive. The wise person recognizes when grace has served its purpose and allows the work to stand unadorned.

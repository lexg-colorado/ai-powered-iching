# Hexagram 19 — ䷒ 臨 (lín) — Approach

**Alternate Names:** "Approach", "Nearing", "Overseeing", "Dominion"

**Upper Trigram:** ☷ 坤 (kūn) — Earth (地)
**Lower Trigram:** ☱ 兌 (duì) — Lake (澤)

**King Wen Number:** 19 | **Binary:** 000011

---

## Overview

Approaching signifies a gradual drawing near of positive influence and favorable circumstance. It represents the culmination of prior cultivation, where benevolent forces close the distance and a person of authority gains proximity and influence over those below.

## Judgment

Approaching brings great fortune and success through proximity of favorable conditions. The superior person in an elevated position beneficially influences those in subordinate stations, creating mutual advantage through clear intention and dignified bearing.

## Image

The Earth above and the Lake below depict accumulation and receptivity meeting. The superior person achieves distinction through countless small acts of virtue combined with receptive attentiveness to others' needs.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

At the foundation, sincere approach to worthiness begins hesitantly but with commitment. Early movements toward elevation bring only small gains, yet consistency establishes momentum.

### Nine in the Second Place

The second line encounters genuine approach and growing influence, with success assured through continued integrity. Others recognize and respond to authentic sincerity.

### Six in the Third Place

As approach deepens, comfort and ease may breed complacency. Remaining vigilant and maintaining standards prevents the erosion that comes from excessive contentment.

### Six in the Fourth Place

At the center, approach reaches its fullness, and one's influence extends widely. This position of authority demands constant awareness of responsibility and moral foundation.

### Six in the Fifth Place

Approach at the fifth line achieves maximum effect through wise governance. The person of power exercises influence with restraint and discernment, gaining loyalty through virtue.

### Six at the Top

At culmination, excessive approach becomes oppressive. The wise person recognizes when nearness must yield to respectful distance, knowing when to withdraw and allow independence.

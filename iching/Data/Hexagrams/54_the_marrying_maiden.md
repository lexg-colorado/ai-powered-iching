# Hexagram 54 — ䷵ 歸妹 (guī mèi) — The Marrying Maiden

**Alternate Names:** "The Marrying Maiden", "Converting the Maiden", "Returning Maiden", "Subordinate"

**Upper Trigram:** ☳ 震 (zhèn) — Thunder (雷)
**Lower Trigram:** ☱ 兌 (duì) — Lake (澤)

**King Wen Number:** 54 | **Binary:** 001011

---

## Overview

Converting the Maiden describes the acceptance of a lesser role within proper hierarchy and relationship. A young woman entering marriage enters subordination as her destined place; yet within this structure lies dignity and purpose. This hexagram speaks to proper alignment and acceptance of one's station.

## Judgment

The maiden succeeds through embracing her role with grace and sincerity. Though positioned below, she fulfills function through virtue and acceptance rather than striving. Her place, though circumscribed, offers opportunity for genuine influence through propriety.

## Image

Thunder rises through Lake in volatility tempered by reflection. The superior woman thus understands the limits of her station and conducts herself with restraint, knowing that lasting benefit comes through harmony rather than assertion.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The maiden takes a secondary position with purpose clear. Though younger in station, she serves with sincerity and finds her place naturally within the larger structure.

### Nine in the Second Place

The maiden waits in the darkness but light approaches. Patience and proper conduct during the waiting period prepare one for what the relationship will require.

### Six in the Third Place

The maiden expects marriage but meets with delay. Frustration at the postponement tests resolve; acceptance and continued virtue ensure proper timing will eventually arrive.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The maiden prolongs her waiting past the proper season. Excessive delay in assuming one's destined role through continued hesitation brings stagnation rather than benefit.

### Six in the Fifth Place

The sovereign gives his daughter in marriage with all honor and ceremony. When one finally enters the role with proper recognition and authentic commitment, true function begins.

### Six at the Top

The maiden takes her place in the household with all duties acknowledged. Arrival at one's destined position, embraced fully and performed with integrity, brings completion and blessing.

# Hexagram 33 — ䷠ 遯 (dùn) — Retreat

**Alternate Names:** "Retreat", "Retiring", "Yielding", "Withdrawal", "Concealment"

**Upper Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)
**Lower Trigram:** ☶ 艮 (gèn) — Mountain (山)

**King Wen Number:** 33 | **Binary:** 111100

---

## Overview

Retreat signifies strategic withdrawal and the wisdom of yielding to superior force. It is not defeat, but the judicious choice to step back when conditions are unfavorable, allowing adverse influences to pass. This is a moment to consolidate inner resources and prepare for a future return.

## Judgment

Retreat is favorable when undertaken with correct timing and clear intention. The superior person benefits by knowing when to withdraw rather than persist foolishly. Small obstacles may delay progress, but the act of retiring preserves one's capacity to advance later.

## Image

Heaven moves upward and mountain rests below, creating separation and withdrawal. This symbolizes the movement of yang force upward and away, leaving receptive influence in its wake.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The tail of retreat calls for caution and awareness of pursuit. Lingering too long invites danger; swift but measured withdrawal prevents entanglement. The inferior may follow, but steadfastness in purpose protects against entanglement.

### Six in the Second Place

Binding retreat with ropes of determination holds one firm during withdrawal. To retreat properly requires strong resolve not to be drawn back; loyalty to one's chosen path prevents the weakness of indecision.

### Nine in the Third Place

Restraint in retreat prevents the confusion of scattered escape. A disciplined withdrawal that maintains order and purpose is superior to panicked flight. The wise retreat in formation, protecting what must be protected.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

Deliberate retreat chosen freely brings good fortune; forced retreat brings misfortune. The superior person withdraws because conditions demand it, not from cowardice. Withdrawal undertaken voluntarily carries the seed of advantage.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

Smooth and graceful retreat occurs when the moment is clearly read. Like a noble who withdraws at the proper season, such timing brings no blame. The entire path of retreat proceeds without hindrance or confusion.

### Nine at the Top

Far-reaching retreat pursued without hesitation or looking back indicates clarity of vision. When one discerns that retreat is necessary and executes it thoroughly, advantage accumulates for the future. This is joyful withdrawal, free from doubt.

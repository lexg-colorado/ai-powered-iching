# Hexagram 42 — ䷩ 益 (yì) — Increase

**Alternate Names:** "Increase", "Augmenting", "Benefit", "Gain"

**Upper Trigram:** ☴ 巽 (xùn) — Wind (風)
**Lower Trigram:** ☳ 震 (zhèn) — Thunder (雷)

**King Wen Number:** 42 | **Binary:** 110001

---

## Overview

Augmenting represents expansion and growth through active cultivation and mutual benefit. Energy flows upward like wind above thunder, multiplying what is sown. This hexagram favors decisive action that benefits both self and others.

## Judgment

Increase brings great fortune when pursued with sincerity and without hesitation. The superior person advances the worthy and strengthens the foundation of the community, knowing that mutual benefit sustains long-term growth.

## Image

Thunder rises beneath the wind, and the wind carries the thunder's energy outward and upward. This spreading of dynamic force symbolizes the multiplication and dissemination of good influence.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The first line counsels making great strides in beneficial directions without doubt. When opportunity for genuine progress appears, hesitation diminishes its value; the moment favors bold commitment.

### Six in the Second Place

The second line describes the blessing that flows to those who give without calculation. Acting with generosity toward others creates conditions where one's own good fortune naturally increases.

### Six in the Third Place

The third line warns that increase arising from selfishness and harm to others invites resentment. Only increase grounded in mutual advantage and ethical conduct brings lasting blessing.

### Six in the Fourth Place

The fourth line emphasizes the power of influence when the heart is resolute and sincere. A leader who genuinely serves the common good finds that all around them rise with equal favor.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The fifth line speaks of supreme good fortune through magnanimous transformation of others. When one uses increase not for personal advantage but to elevate the community, all benefit together.

### Nine at the Top

The sixth line cautions that attacking and expanding without restraint invites collapse. The culmination of increase must be tempered by wisdom; endless growth without foundation leads to ruin.

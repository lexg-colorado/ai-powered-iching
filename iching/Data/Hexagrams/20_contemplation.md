# Hexagram 20 — ䷓ 觀 (guān) — Contemplation

**Alternate Names:** "Contemplation", "Viewing", "Observation", "Looking"

**Upper Trigram:** ☴ 巽 (xùn) — Wind (風)
**Lower Trigram:** ☷ 坤 (kūn) — Earth (地)

**King Wen Number:** 20 | **Binary:** 110000

---

## Overview

Contemplation emphasizes the power of observation and interpretation, where understanding arises through patient examination of phenomena and their patterns. It suggests that genuine insight develops when one cultivates receptive awareness rather than forceful intervention.

## Judgment

Contemplation brings advantage through the quality of one's observation and the sincerity of intention in seeking understanding. The person who observes widely yet focuses deeply gains authentic knowledge and the respect of those who recognize their clarity.

## Image

Wind spreading across the Earth suggests broad circulation and universal perception. The superior person contemplates the seasons and patterns of nature, thereby understanding the principles that govern all transformations.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

At the foundation, the observer glimpses only surface appearances and partial views. A child's or foreigner's perspective, limited yet innocent, offers beginning awareness without presumption of mastery.

### Six in the Second Place

Observing the patterns within one's own community provides understanding rooted in particular context. Domestic or local insight yields practical wisdom through intimate familiarity.

### Six in the Third Place

Expanding observation to encompass the larger cycles and patterns reveals deeper meaning. The contemplative person discerns the will and direction of fate through careful reading of signs.

### Six in the Fourth Place

At the center of vision, one gains sufficient perspective to guide others and influence outcomes. Yet this clarity of sight brings responsibility to share understanding with integrity.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

Observing the sacred dimension of existence—the patterns that transcend personal interest—elevates consciousness. The wise person sees the universal principles working through particular instances.

### Nine at the Top

At culmination, the observer transcends individual perspective and recognizes themselves as observed. The profound contemplative understands that awareness itself participates in and shapes what is perceived.

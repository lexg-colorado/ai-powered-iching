# Hexagram 14 — ䷍ 大有 (dà yǒu) — Possession in Great Measure

**Alternate Names:** "Possession in Great Measure", "Great Possession", "Great Having", "Abundance", "Great Resources"

**Upper Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)
**Lower Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)

**King Wen Number:** 14 | **Binary:** 101111

---

## Overview

Great Possessing represents the accumulation of wealth and resources through natural virtue and favorable circumstance. Like fire rising to heaven, this hexagram shows abundance recognized and properly used. The emphasis falls not on acquisition but on wise stewardship of what naturally comes.

## Judgment

Great abundance flows to those whose virtue attracts it naturally and whose wisdom ensures proper use. Success comes through openness and generosity in sharing resources; those who grasp tightly invite loss, while those who distribute wisely maintain and increase their store. Humility preserves fortune.

## Image

Fire beneath heaven radiates warmth and light in all directions, illuminating and nurturing all things. Great possession works similarly, its value measured not by hoarding but by beneficial influence and generous distribution.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The first line possesses resources but lacks confidence or awareness of their value. Simple care and modest use prevent loss; avoiding both wastefulness and hoarding maintains steady benefit from what one naturally possesses.

### Nine in the Second Place

The second line acquires additional resources through continuing virtue. Strength and capability increase; the wise remain humble despite growing means and continue supporting others as capacity allows.

### Nine in the Third Place

The third line encounters the challenge of excessive possession, where abundance may breed carelessness. Distinguishing between genuine need and false desire becomes crucial; proper restraint now prevents loss through indulgence.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The fourth line moves from private possession toward position of public stewardship. Resources that were personal now become trust responsibility; humility and fairness in distribution consolidate power through gaining genuine loyalty.

### Six in the Fifth Place

The fifth line holds central authority with abundant resources at command. This position allows magnificent expression of virtue; great works benefit many, and the wise ensure that abundance serves worthy purpose beyond personal glory.

### Nine at the Top

The sixth line shows possession at its zenith, carrying the danger of reversal. Even unlimited resources cannot prevent consequences of arrogance; the wise remain conscious that possession is temporary trust, not permanent possession.

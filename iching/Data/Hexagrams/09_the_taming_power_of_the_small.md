# Hexagram 9 — ䷈ 小畜 (xiǎo xù) — The Taming Power of the Small

**Alternate Names:** "The Taming Power of the Small", "Small Harvest", "Minor Restraint", "Domestication", "Small Holding Back"

**Upper Trigram:** ☴ 巽 (xùn) — Wind (風)
**Lower Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)

**King Wen Number:** 9 | **Binary:** 110111

---

## Overview

The image of wind accumulating gently against heaven suggests modest restraint and gradual accumulation of influence. This hexagram describes a period when subtle forces gather slowly, not through force but through persistence and careful attention. Success comes through patience and respect for limitations.

## Judgment

Small Accumulating requires persistent gentleness and restraint rather than aggressive expansion. Progress is steady but modest, achieved through loyalty and attention to detail rather than grand gestures. Fortune favors those who respect boundaries and work within present constraints.

## Image

Wind moves across heaven, circling and gathering without direct force. This suggests the accumulation of small efforts over time, building power through consistency and natural persistence.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The first line represents beginning restraint, where one encounters the first boundary or obstacle. Proceeding with awareness of limitation preserves future progress and prevents premature exhaustion of resources.

### Nine in the Second Place

The second line shows voluntary acceptance of constraint, finding harmony within bounds. This inner alignment with necessity transforms limitation from oppression into natural order, creating solid foundation for gradual growth.

### Nine in the Third Place

The third line approaches the threshold where restraint becomes difficult to maintain. Tension increases as desire presses against limit; wise judgment here prevents rupture and maintains the accumulating momentum.

### Six in the Fourth Place

The fourth line enters the sphere of power but remains in service position. Approaching authority with continued humility and restraint ensures continued favor and allows genuine influence to develop without resentment.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The fifth line holds central authority with accumulated credibility. True leadership here manifests as shared blessing and mutual benefit, where small restraint now carries the weight of collective trust.

### Nine at the Top

The sixth line represents danger of pursuing small accumulation beyond its natural limit. Continuing restraint when conditions call for release brings stagnation; the time comes to release what has been gathered.

# Hexagram 10 — ䷉ 履 (lǚ) — Treading

**Alternate Names:** "Treading", "Conduct", "Continuing", "Stepping", "Walking the Path"

**Upper Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)
**Lower Trigram:** ☱ 兌 (duì) — Lake (澤)

**King Wen Number:** 10 | **Binary:** 111011

---

## Overview

Treading represents moving forward with conscious attention to proper conduct and right relationship. Like walking carefully on thin ice, success depends on respectful acknowledgment of differences and careful navigation of existing conditions. The hexagram emphasizes propriety and awareness of context.

## Judgment

Treading successfully requires awareness of one's place and respectful conduct toward those above and below. Even when moving among those of superior position or difficult circumstances, persistence in propriety brings smooth passage. The key is adaptation without loss of principle.

## Image

Heaven above and joyful water below show the gentle but firm movement across varied terrain. Treading represents the way one moves through life with awareness, neither reckless nor timid, respecting the natural order.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The first line describes simple direct treading, without complication. Honest, straightforward action proceeding from humble position meets no resistance; simplicity itself becomes protection.

### Nine in the Second Place

The second line shows treading the middle way, balanced and self-contained. This position possesses inner steadiness and modest confidence; solitary progress is safe because motivation remains pure.

### Six in the Third Place

The third line encounters the threshold of difficulty, where awareness of position becomes crucial. One must recognize limitations and proceed carefully; overconfidence here invites danger from those with greater power.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The fourth line enters proximity to authority, requiring heightened sensitivity. Caution and respectful deference are necessary; this position is inherently precarious and success depends on maintained awareness.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The fifth line holds central position among those of greater rank. Clear vision and firm resolve allow proper judgment about boundaries; wielding authority with restraint demonstrates true capability.

### Nine at the Top

The sixth line shows treading carried to completion or excess. Having successfully navigated all positions, one must now step back and examine the path traveled; the journey is complete when awareness itself becomes the teaching.

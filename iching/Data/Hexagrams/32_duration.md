# Hexagram 32 — ䷟ 恆 (héng) — Duration

**Alternate Names:** "Duration", "Persevering", "Constancy", "Endurance", "Continuity"

**Upper Trigram:** ☳ 震 (zhèn) — Thunder (雷)
**Lower Trigram:** ☴ 巽 (xùn) — Wind (風)

**King Wen Number:** 32 | **Binary:** 001110

---

## Overview

Thunder above Wind represents perpetual motion in complementary directions, neither tiring nor changing course. This hexagram embodies the principle of lasting order through sustained effort and unwavering commitment. What endures does so not through force but through the rhythm of natural patterns.

## Judgment

Perseverance in the proper path brings success that outlasts all obstacles. The superior person understands that constancy is not rigidity but the faithful return to right action through all seasons. Duration requires correct method and the discipline to maintain it.

## Image

Thunder and wind move together without contradiction, each sustaining the other's motion. The image teaches that lasting order arises when complementary forces work in harmony, neither forcing the other to change.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The first line, firm at the base, shows one beginning a commitment to duration. The foundation is set; vigilance prevents deviation from the outset. This is the position of the initiator of constancy.

### Nine in the Second Place

The second line, yielding and central, indicates one whose steadiness supports what endures. Though yielding, one does not waver. This position brings good fortune through faithful service.

### Nine in the Third Place

The third line, yielding unbalanced, depicts one whose constancy becomes inconsistency. Shame and disgrace follow from abandoning the path. This position warns that even half-hearted commitment ends in loss.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The fourth line, firm and rising, shows one who seeks permanence but finds only change around them. No game is found in the field; nothing responds to effort. This position teaches that some things cannot be forced to endure.

### Six in the Fifth Place

The fifth line, yielding in authority, represents one who perseveres in accepting what comes. Without imposing rigid expectations, one remains constant through all phases. This is the position of the sage of duration.

### Six at the Top

The sixth line, firm at the extremity, shows one whose perseverance has become obsession. Restlessness sets in even at the point of victory. This position teaches that perpetual motion without wisdom eventually exhausts itself.

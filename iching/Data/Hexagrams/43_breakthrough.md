# Hexagram 43 — ䷪ 夬 (guài) — Break-through

**Alternate Names:** "Break-through", "Resoluteness", "Parting", "Displacement"

**Upper Trigram:** ☱ 兌 (duì) — Lake (澤)
**Lower Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)

**King Wen Number:** 43 | **Binary:** 011111

---

## Overview

Parting represents the decisive moment when the inferior must be separated from the superior. Though gentle water rests below, the strong lake above stands firm and immovable. This hexagram urges unwavering resolve when corrupt elements must be removed.

## Judgment

Break-through favors the resolute who move with certainty against entrenched disorder. The time for half-measures has passed; clear action by those of integrity disperses what is false, though confrontation may be necessary.

## Image

The lake stands elevated and overflows, and its waters sweep away what lies beneath. This torrent of accumulated pressure releases what has long been blocked, clearing the way forward.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The first line warns against precipitous action driven by anger or impatience. The strongest break-through begins with the toes; forcing the entire structure too quickly invites backlash and failure.

### Nine in the Second Place

The second line cautions that necessary parting becomes regrettable when pursued with resentment or malice. Act with resolve but without bitterness; the goal is removal of harm, not punishment.

### Nine in the Third Place

The third line describes exhaustion that comes from sustained effort against resistance. After pushing through obstacles, rest and recovery are needed; continued strain without respite leads to diminishment.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The fourth line emphasizes that decisive action taken with sincerity and without hesitation brings good fortune. When the moment of parting arrives, conviction and swift movement prevent the inferior from regrouping.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The fifth line speaks of great good fortune when the superior leads the parting with benevolence. Those of high station who remove disorder while caring for the welfare of all gain loyal support.

### Six at the Top

The sixth line warns that pursuing parting beyond its natural conclusion brings remorse. Once the breach is made and the inferior separated, further conflict serves no purpose and dissipates one's power.

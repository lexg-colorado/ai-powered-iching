# Hexagram 62 — ䷽ 小過 (xiǎo guò) — Preponderance of the Small

**Alternate Names:** "Preponderance of the Small", "Small Exceeding", "Small Surpassing", "Slight Excess", "Minor Transgression"

**Upper Trigram:** ☳ 震 (zhèn) — Thunder (雷)
**Lower Trigram:** ☶ 艮 (gèn) — Mountain (山)

**King Wen Number:** 62 | **Binary:** 001100

---

## Overview

Thunder over Mountain shows the small surpassing or exceeding its natural bounds, creating a situation of minor imbalance. This hexagram represents the tension when details outweigh the whole or smaller matters become disproportionately significant. Skill lies in managing this delicate asymmetry without losing overall integrity.

## Judgment

Small matters require special care when they exceed their proper scope. This is a time for meticulous attention and incremental rather than bold action. Success depends upon respecting limits while addressing what is genuinely out of balance.

## Image

Thunder moves over the mountain with intensity. The superior person thereby handles small matters with great care and avoids careless transgression.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

Small excess at the start seems harmless yet sets a pattern. The superior person catches minor deviations before they compound. Attending to trifles now prevents serious disorder later.

### Six in the Second Place

When circumspection becomes timidity, one fails to act when action is needed. The challenge is to remain careful without becoming paralyzed. Small courage applied rightly corrects small imbalances.

### Nine in the Third Place

The center struggles with the tension of this hexagram. Too much attention to minutiae obscures the greater purpose. One must keep perspective and not let the small obscure the essential.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

Small transgressions can be overlooked if not repeated. Yet when they become habitual, they undermine character and trust. The time calls for correcting course while the deviation remains minor.

### Six in the Fifth Place

When minor matters escalate despite careful attention, caution increases. Not all small issues yield easily; some require sustained discipline. Persistence in small corrections achieves great results.

### Six at the Top

Exceeding bounds in minor matters finally breaks through into major consequences. What was controllable becomes dangerous through sustained excess. The time demands decisive action to return to proper limits.

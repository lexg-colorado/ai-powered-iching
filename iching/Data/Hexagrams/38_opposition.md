# Hexagram 38 — ䷥ 睽 (kuí) — Opposition

**Alternate Names:** "Opposition", "Polarising", "Estrangement", "Divergence", "Diverging Paths"

**Upper Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)
**Lower Trigram:** ☱ 兌 (duì) — Lake (澤)

**King Wen Number:** 38 | **Binary:** 101011

---

## Overview

Opposition signifies the natural separation and divergence of interests that arise when unlike elements interact. Yet even amid estrangement, small good fortune persists through proper conduct. This hexagram reveals that opposition is not inherently destructive but requires careful navigation and recognition of genuine points of contact that transcend apparent differences.

## Judgment

Opposition brings small good fortune when the superior person recognizes hidden harmony beneath apparent discord. While agreement is preferable, benefit can arise from opposition when one acts with sincerity and fairness. Understanding the legitimate basis of difference prevents escalation to irreversible conflict.

## Image

Fire rises while lake descends; their movements are opposite yet both proceed according to their nature. This symbolizes forces in essential opposition yet each fulfilling their proper function without mutual destruction.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

Regret fades when one recognizes opposition as temporary rather than permanent estrangement. Initial confusion about divided interests gradually clarifies into realistic understanding. The superior person seeks small points of agreement even amid fundamental difference.

### Nine in the Second Place

Opposition leading to chance encounter with the sovereign brings unexpected advantage and reconciliation. In circumstances of seeming separation, timely meeting can dissolve misunderstanding. Sincere intention sometimes finds receptiveness even in opposition.

### Six in the Third Place

Opposition and estrangement create the appearance of betrayal by close companions, yet the superior person sees through this to underlying loyalty. Appearances deceive; the genuinely faithful sometimes seem distant due to circumstance. Maintaining trust through apparent opposition proves wisdom.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

Isolation brings bewilderment and confusion yet also cleanses the mind of misconception. Complete separation, though painful, sometimes clears away false attachments and restores clarity. Eventual meeting after estrangement carries particular poignancy and truth.

### Six in the Fifth Place

Regret eventually vanishes when the source of opposition is clearly recognized and addressed. Meeting with the sovereign after separation symbolizes alignment of intention despite difference. Obstacles that seemed permanent dissolve when underlying cause is understood.

### Nine at the Top

Opposition ceasing abruptly as if through external force brings transformation and cautious happiness. Sometimes opposition dissolves not through direct effort but through circumstance. The superior person greets such change with measured joy, maintaining awareness and integrity.

# Hexagram 36 — ䷣ 明夷 (míng yí) — Darkening of the Light

**Alternate Names:** "Darkening of the Light", "Brightness Hiding", "Intelligence Wounded", "Brilliance Injured", "The Darkened Light"

**Upper Trigram:** ☷ 坤 (kūn) — Earth (地)
**Lower Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)

**King Wen Number:** 36 | **Binary:** 000101

---

## Overview

Darkening of the Light represents the obscuration of clarity by hostile circumstances and the necessity of inner illumination amid external gloom. Virtue and wisdom are not eliminated but must be safeguarded through concealment and patience. This is the time for the superior person to maintain inner brightness while adapting outwardly to unfavorable conditions.

## Judgment

During darkening of the light, the superior person advances inwardly while maintaining outer adaptation. Progress comes through preserving one's integrity rather than bold action. Benefit lies in recognizing the concealment of brightness and responding with humble discretion until conditions shift.

## Image

Fire descends into the earth, its light becoming hidden and obscured. This symbolizes the dimming of clarity, the hiding of virtue, and the necessity of inward cultivation during outer adversity.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The superior person rises at first light before darkness overwhelms the world. Early recognition of approaching gloom and swift adjustment prevent being caught unprepared. Awareness and timely action preserve what might otherwise be lost to shadow.

### Six in the Second Place

Wounding on the left thigh allows continued movement forward, though with injury and limitation. The superior person endures minor harm while maintaining essential function and purpose. Partial injury, borne with steadiness, does not prevent eventual progress.

### Nine in the Third Place

Hunting in the south brings great fortune when the true enemy is recognized. Clarity of purpose amid obscuration allows the superior person to identify and address the real source of difficulty. Strategic action, rightly directed, succeeds even in darkness.

### Six in the Fourth Place

Entering the left belly penetrates the darkening force's hidden intention. Understanding the nature of adverse circumstances from within, without being consumed by them, brings awareness of the path forward. Deep perception amidst obscurity offers peculiar advantage.

### Six in the Fifth Place

The darkness of Ji Tzu (a wise ancient whose brightness was hidden) signifies virtue enduring unseen. The superior person's essential worth continues undiminished even when outer recognition is withdrawn. Inner light persists in secret, awaiting future revelation.

### Six at the Top

First there is darkness, then emergence into light brings eventual advantage. The complete cycle of obscuration followed by clarification symbolizes the restoration of conditions favorable to virtue. Patience through the full darkening ensures the benefit of eventual radiance.

# Hexagram 55 — ䷶ 豐 (fēng) — Abundance

**Alternate Names:** "Abundance", "Abounding", "Fullness", "Plenty"

**Upper Trigram:** ☳ 震 (zhèn) — Thunder (雷)
**Lower Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)

**King Wen Number:** 55 | **Binary:** 001101

---

## Overview

Abundance represents a time of great fullness and flourishing potential. When conditions ripen and resources multiply, the challenge becomes managing prosperity wisely. This hexagram warns that the very fullness that brings joy can cloud judgment if not handled with clear vision.

## Judgment

Abundance brings success, yet only when the superior person remains centered amid plenty. The challenge is not to attract abundance but to maintain clarity and purpose when surroundings burst with excess. Discipline becomes more essential, not less, in times of fullness.

## Image

Thunder radiates lightning, illuminating all things at their peak. The superior person thus stands at the height of her power and scrutinizes herself vigilantly, ensuring that abundance does not obscure proper judgment.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

Meeting a master and finding abundance through connection begins well. When guidance and right relationship are established, prosperity may follow naturally and appropriately.

### Six in the Second Place

The abundance increases and curtains of light obscure the fuller view. As richness deepens, clarity becomes harder to maintain; seek advisors and check your course frequently against true measure.

### Nine in the Third Place

The abundance conceals the inner view with screens and shadows. Excess blocks vision of what truly matters; the danger lies not in having too much but in losing sight of authentic purpose amid the pleasant obscuration.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The abundance peaks and begins to wane; the day of fullness approaches its natural decline. Recognize that all times of plenty contain within them the seeds of return to balance; maintain dignity and purpose as conditions shift.

### Six in the Fifth Place

Light comes through the clouds and honors approach. Though circumstances constrain the outward display of abundance, inner worth is recognized by those who perceive correctly.

### Six at the Top

The abundance reaches its utmost but threatens to become burden rather than blessing. At the peak, recognize that continuation of present form becomes impossible; prepare for natural return to balance without loss of core value.

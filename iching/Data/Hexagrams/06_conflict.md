# Hexagram 6 — ䷅ 訟 (sòng) — Conflict

**Alternate Names:** "Conflict", "Lawsuit", "Dispute", "Argumentation", "Contention"

**Upper Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)
**Lower Trigram:** ☵ 坎 (kǎn) — Water (水)

**King Wen Number:** 6 | **Binary:** 111010

---

## Overview

Hexagram of opposing forces and their inevitable collision. Heaven and water move in contrary directions, creating friction and discord. Represents conflicts born from genuine opposition, the danger of litigation, and the necessity of seeking resolution before escalation.

## Judgment

Conflict finds limited success; constancy in the middle brings fortune, but ultimate victory is not assured. Seek reconciliation through sincerity rather than prolonging dispute. The wise recognize limits and retreat before complete engagement to prevent grave harm.

## Image

Water returning upward against heaven conveys fundamental opposition. The superior person examines conflict carefully from its inception, turning back at the threshold before full entanglement.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

Not persisting in the case—small talk and laughter end the matter. The beginning of conflict may be dissolved through levity and modest speech, avoiding formalization of dispute. Early intervention prevents escalation.

### Nine in the Second Place

Unable to engage in the lawsuit—return to estate of 300 families brings fortune. The middle yin withdraws from contention despite provocation, accepting loss gracefully. Retreat and acceptance prevent greater disaster.

### Six in the Third Place

Nourished by the labors of ancestors—persevering in difficulty comes to completion. Taking sustenance from past virtue, one endures hardship with constancy. The conflict transforms into patient, supported effort toward resolution.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

Unable to engage in the lawsuit—return to heaven's decree brings constancy. Surrender the case to higher authority; accepting fate and divine judgment avoids further striving. Peace comes through releasing the struggle.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

Conflict in the great court—supreme fortune through the center. The higher authority judges impartially; truthful presentation before the superior brings vindication and a just resolution.

### Nine at the Top

Even if granted the belt of rank, three times before dawn it will be stripped away. Victory at any cost brings ruinous reversal. The extreme of conflict overturns gain; no permanent triumph comes from warfare, only exhaustion.

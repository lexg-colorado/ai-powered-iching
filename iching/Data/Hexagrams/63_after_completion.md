# Hexagram 63 — ䷾ 既濟 (jì jì) — After Completion

**Alternate Names:** "After Completion", "Already Fording", "Already Across", "Settled", "Accomplished"

**Upper Trigram:** ☵ 坎 (kǎn) — Water (水)
**Lower Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)

**King Wen Number:** 63 | **Binary:** 010101

---

## Overview

Water over Fire represents a state where the great work is complete and one has crossed to the other side. This hexagram marks fulfillment and the challenge of transition that follows success. The crossing itself is over; now comes the task of maintaining what has been achieved.

## Judgment

When the goal is reached, vigilance must not cease but transform into maintenance. Success invites complacency, which breeds ruin. The superior person recognizes that completion is not ending but transition to new careful stewardship.

## Image

Water stands over fire, each in its proper place. The superior person thereby reflects on misfortune and takes precautions against it.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The crossing is complete; one has reached solid ground. Remaining alert prevents being pulled back into the stream. The beginning of the new phase requires as much care as the transition itself.

### Six in the Second Place

At rest after effort, one must attend to lingering matters. The crossing is done but loose threads remain to be secured. Small attentions prevent small problems from growing.

### Nine in the Third Place

Having crossed successfully, pride can lead to recklessness. Continued progress from this new position requires the same respect for process one learned on the journey. Complacency now invites reversal.

### Six in the Fourth Place

Near the culmination, wisdom reviews what has been accomplished. Understanding how the crossing was made provides insight for future endeavors. The superior person learns from completion.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The transformation from struggle to stability is delicate. While the crossing is complete, one must not immediately declare victory. Steady attention to the new conditions ensures lasting settlement.

### Six at the Top

Once fully established in the new state, one risks becoming overconfident. History suggests that what has been achieved can be lost through neglect. The truest success is to remain humble and observant even after victory.

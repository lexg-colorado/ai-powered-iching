# Hexagram 37 — ䷤ 家人 (jiā rén) — The Family

**Alternate Names:** "The Family", "Dwelling People", "The Clan", "Household", "Family Members"

**Upper Trigram:** ☴ 巽 (xùn) — Wind (風)
**Lower Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)

**King Wen Number:** 37 | **Binary:** 110101

---

## Overview

The Family represents the fundamental order arising from proper relationships within the home and the natural hierarchy of roles within domestic life. Like a well-ordered household, society flourishes when each member fulfills their place with sincerity and respect. This hexagram teaches that the family is the foundation for all larger social structures and personal virtue.

## Judgment

The Family is fortunate when each member accepts and honors their role with genuine commitment. Strength in the household comes not from dominance but from each person—from the most senior to the youngest—maintaining integrity in their station. True order emerges from inner sincerity rather than imposed rules.

## Image

Wind rises from fire, spreading warmth and order throughout the dwelling. This symbolizes the proper distribution of roles and affection that creates harmonious family life, each person contributing to the whole.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The establishment of family begins with clear household structure and respect for proper boundaries. When the foundation is set with clarity about roles and expectations, difficulties are prevented before they arise. Early establishment of order prevents chaos and conflict.

### Six in the Second Place

The wife manages the inner household while the husband manages external affairs; each fulfilling their role without blame. When roles are clear and complementary, mutual respect flourishes naturally. This balance brings subtle good fortune through harmony of function.

### Nine in the Third Place

Severity in the household, though sometimes harsh, prevents disorder and maintains family cohesion. Firm discipline tempered with justice keeps all in proper alignment. Strictness used righteously preserves the family when gentleness alone would permit dissolution.

### Six in the Fourth Place

The wife brings prosperity to the household through faithful management of resources and care. Prosperity within the home arises from inner devotion and capable stewardship. The security and abundance of the domestic sphere rests on steadfast responsibility.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The sovereign brings grace to the household through benevolent oversight and sincere concern. When the head of the family leads with integrity and genuine care, all respond with loyalty and diligence. Leadership rooted in compassion creates voluntary obedience.

### Nine at the Top

Genuine sincerity in family bonds brings ultimate good fortune and eventual grandeur. When all relations rest on authentic care and righteous conduct rather than mere obligation, the family becomes a source of strength and blessing. Such genuine dwelling together creates lasting stability and growth.

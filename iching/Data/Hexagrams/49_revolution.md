# Hexagram 49 — ䷰ 革 (gé) — Revolution

**Alternate Names:** "Revolution", "Skinning", "Molting", "Radical Change"

**Upper Trigram:** ☱ 兌 (duì) — Lake (澤)
**Lower Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)

**King Wen Number:** 49 | **Binary:** 011101

---

## Overview

Revolution represents transformation through necessary change. When conditions demand fundamental renewal, the sage must have courage to abandon the old and establish the new. This hexagram speaks to the moment when reform becomes inevitable and beneficial.

## Judgment

Revolution succeeds when the people are ready and the time is ripe. The wise leader recognizes when incremental adjustment has failed and bold transformation is required. This change, though disruptive, brings renewed vigor and proper order.

## Image

Fire beneath the Lake consumes what was established and rises anew. The superior person thus renews the calendar and reshapes institutions in harmony with the changing seasons.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The first change stirs in secret. One should observe conditions carefully before moving, ensuring the ground is truly prepared for transformation.

### Six in the Second Place

When change is necessary, swift action at the proper moment bears fruit. Hesitation or half-measures invite discord; full commitment to the new way is required.

### Nine in the Third Place

The wise leader moves cautiously, seeking counsel and moving gradually. Premature haste or excessive force in revolution creates new disorder rather than true renewal.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

Change must be supported by those affected. When conviction is established and the people follow willingly, the transformation is complete and lasting.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The superior person embodies the change gracefully, neither clinging to the past nor forcing the future. Inner alignment with the transformation ensures natural and harmonious progress.

### Six at the Top

The old form dissolves into the new. Having surrendered what was, one must now cultivate patience and faith in the emerging order without doubt or second-guessing.

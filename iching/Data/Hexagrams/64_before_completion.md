# Hexagram 64 — ䷿ 未濟 (wèi jì) — Before Completion

**Alternate Names:** "Before Completion", "Not Yet Fording", "Not Yet Across", "Unsettled", "Incomplete"

**Upper Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)
**Lower Trigram:** ☵ 坎 (kǎn) — Water (水)

**King Wen Number:** 64 | **Binary:** 101010

---

## Overview

Fire over Water represents the moment before crossing, when all preparations are made but the goal remains ahead. This hexagram embodies the tension of potential unfulfilled and the necessity of continued effort. The situation is not yet settled; consciousness and exertion remain essential.

## Judgment

Completion is near but not yet achieved; the supreme effort must not flag. This hexagram favors renewed commitment and clear vision of the goal. Progress depends upon maintaining direction and resolve until the crossing is truly complete.

## Image

Fire reaches upward while water flows downward; their natures oppose. The superior person thereby distinguishes the beginning from the end and prepares for the crossing.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

The beginning of the journey shows promise yet uncertainty. One must discern the true path and commit to the effort. Hesitation or doubt at this stage invites unnecessary difficulty.

### Nine in the Second Place

As effort continues, adjustment of methods becomes necessary. Rigidity in approach often prevents progress more than obstacles do. Flexibility coupled with determination advances the crossing.

### Six in the Third Place

The midpoint reveals how far one has come and how far remains. This is not the place for doubt but for steady accumulation of progress. Maintaining course and pace is essential.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

Approaching the goal brings both hope and hazard. Many fail near the end through carelessness or changed commitment. The superior person redoubles attention as the crossing nears.

### Six in the Fifth Place

Almost across, one can nearly glimpse the far shore. Yet this proximity can breed complacency that invites slipping back. The final effort must be as strong as the first.

### Nine at the Top

The crossing is complete for those who maintain vision and effort. Those who falter near the end find the journey repeats. Success belongs to those who cross fully and do not turn back before the goal.

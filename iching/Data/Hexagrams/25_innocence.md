# Hexagram 25 — ䷘ 無妄 (wú wàng) — Innocence

**Alternate Names:** "Innocence", "Without Embroiling", "The Unexpected", "Fidelity", "Undeluded"

**Upper Trigram:** ☰ 乾 (qián) — Heaven (天)
**Lower Trigram:** ☳ 震 (zhèn) — Thunder (雷)

**King Wen Number:** 25 | **Binary:** 111001

---

## Overview

Heaven above Thunder below represents the ascendant force of pure activity meeting divine order. This hexagram depicts the state of authentic spontaneity, where one remains uncomplicated by artifice and undistorted by false intent. Wrongdoing enters only when innocence is abandoned.

## Judgment

Heaven's movement is constant and true; Thunder responds without hesitation. The superior person aligns with this uncorrupted pattern, advancing in clarity of purpose. Misfortune comes only to those who entangle themselves in delusion or deviation from the natural way.

## Image

Heaven in perpetual motion with Thunder beneath speaks to the unobstructed activity of the Dao. The image teaches constant vigilance against complication and the maintenance of original sincerity.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The first line, firm and at the base, indicates the beginning of proper conduct. One who acts without calculation at this stage lays the foundation for authentic progress. Restraint from ambition ensures no breach of integrity.

### Six in the Second Place

The second line, yielding and central, reflects one who receives instruction readily. Encountering what comes without resistance, the seeker remains unharmed despite external pressures. This is the position of the person who listens well.

### Six in the Third Place

The third line, firm and rising, marks a critical threshold where innocence is tested. Misfortune may come not from one's own error but from circumstance beyond control. One must accept this as part of the natural order.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The fourth line, yielding and approaching the ruler, speaks of one who preserves integrity within constraints. Though confined, the sincere heart remains undamaged. This is the position of the cautious guardian.

### Nine in the Fifth Place

The fifth line, firm and in authority, represents the ruler who embodies innocence. When leadership flows from uncorrupted intent, all follows naturally without force. This is the supreme position of authentic order.

### Nine at the Top

The sixth line, yielding at the summit, shows innocence attempting to act on the world. Yet action here breeds entanglement; the wise refrain. This position teaches that some stages call for stillness rather than engagement.

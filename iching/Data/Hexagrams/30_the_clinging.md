# Hexagram 30 — ䷝ 離 (lí) — The Clinging

**Alternate Names:** "The Clinging", "Radiance", "Fire", "Brightness", "Clarity"

**Upper Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)
**Lower Trigram:** ☲ 離 (lí) — Fire (火)

**King Wen Number:** 30 | **Binary:** 101101

---

## Overview

Fire doubled reveals light that illuminates all things yet cannot exist without its fuel. This hexagram represents clarity, consciousness, and the interdependence of all that shines. What is bright brings vision; what clings brings sustenance. Separation leads to darkness and extinction.

## Judgment

Brightness spreads when properly tended, illuminating all that is hidden. The superior person seeks truth with constant attention, recognizing that clarity requires continuous effort and proper support. What clings survives; what stands alone perishes.

## Image

Fire rising continuously needs constant fuel to maintain its brilliance. The image teaches that understanding requires nourishment and that isolation extinguishes even the brightest flame.

## The Lines

### Nine at the Beginning

The first line, yielding at the base, shows one beginning to perceive light. Yet the steps are confused and unclear. This is the position of early awakening, when sight is still clouded.

### Six in the Second Place

The second line, yielding and central, indicates one firmly grasping the light. Through steady observation and inner truth, clarity deepens. This position brings good fortune through patient illumination.

### Nine in the Third Place

The third line, yielding unbalanced, depicts one whose light becomes unstable and harsh. What shines too brightly casts dark shadows; what burns too hot consumes its fuel. This position warns against excess of brightness.

### Nine in the Fourth Place

The fourth line, firm and rising, shows one whose brilliance affects others. Yet the position approaches the extreme; maintaining the light grows increasingly difficult. This is the position of tested clarity.

### Six in the Fifth Place

The fifth line, firm in authority, represents one whose truth illuminates widely. Others gather around this brightness naturally. This position brings blessings through authentic enlightenment.

### Nine at the Top

The sixth line, yielding at the summit, shows one who rides the fire itself. The perspective becomes cosmic; individual concerns dissolve. This position transcends the personal and touches the eternal.

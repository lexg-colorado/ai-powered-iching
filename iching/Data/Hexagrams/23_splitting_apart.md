# Hexagram 23 — ䷖ 剝 (bō) — Splitting Apart

**Alternate Names:** "Splitting Apart", "Stripping", "Peeling", "Erosion", "Decay"

**Upper Trigram:** ☶ 艮 (gèn) — Mountain (山)
**Lower Trigram:** ☷ 坤 (kūn) — Earth (地)

**King Wen Number:** 23 | **Binary:** 100000

---

## Overview

Splitting Apart represents the natural and inevitable process of deterioration, where what has been built gradually loses coherence and integrity. It teaches acceptance of necessary dissolution and the wisdom of yielding rather than resisting forces beyond individual control.

## Judgment

Splitting Apart brings no advantage to the ambitious, yet understanding its movement brings patience and correct positioning. The wise person recognizes when decline is systemic and yields gracefully, preserving personal integrity even as external structures crumble.

## Image

The Mountain erodes as Earth gradually breaks beneath it, pulled downward by the weight of time. The superior person carefully manages resources and withdraws from deteriorating positions, saving strength for renewal to come.

## The Lines

### Six at the Beginning

At the foundation, small cracks and minor losses begin the process of dissolution. Early erosion, though slight, warns of more substantial decay approaching if nothing arrests the process.

### Six in the Second Place

The splitting extends upward as instability progresses. Those dependent on deteriorating structures face increasing risk and must assess their own position and security.

### Six in the Third Place

At the third position, the framework itself becomes compromised and fails to support its contents. Holding to what is breaking brings entanglement; timely release preserves one's own integrity.

### Six in the Fourth Place

Deterioration of even the elevated areas signals that nothing remains exempt from the process. Faces that adorned the structure now fall away, exposing nakedness and vulnerability.

### Six in the Fifth Place

At the fifth line, the highest reaches still hold, yet the foundation is nearly gone. Clinging to the top position when collapse below makes it untenable leads only to catastrophic failure.

### Nine at the Top

At culmination, what has split apart completely requires no further effort at holding. True understanding comes when one sees that dissolution creates space for eventual renewal.
